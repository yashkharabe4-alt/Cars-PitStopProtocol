 Round 1. Inside Out -- "Debug the

Mind"

This could be really fun and different.
Theme: The central control system of the mind has malfunctioned. Teams must restore
different systems.

Phase 1 -- Memory

Teams are shown technical information/code/data for a limited time.
Then it disappears.
They answer questions based on what they remember.

Phase 2 -- Logic

They receive conflicting instructions and must determine the correct sequence.

Phase 3 -- Core Memory

They receive several outputs from the previous phases and use them to reconstruct the final
"core memory."
Tech: Memory + logic + pattern recognition
Difficulty: Easy

Round 2. Cars -- "Pit Stop Protocol"
This would be much more fun than a normal coding round.

Phase 1 -- Qualifying

Technical MCQs determine starting position.

Phase 2 -- Pit Stop

Teams receive buggy code representing a car's systems:
Fuel
Brake
Engine
Temperature
Speed
They must identify and fix faults.

Phase 3 -- Final Race

Each correctly solved technical problem moves their virtual car forward.
First team to finish wins.
Tech: Debugging + logic + basic programming

Round 3. Finding Nemo -- "Find the

 Final Round. How To Train Your
        Dragon

         Teams face a large digital dragon with a fixed amount of health or XP. The only way to
                  damage the dragon is by solving programming challenges.

         Different difficulty levels deal different amounts of damage. For example:
         Easy LeetCode  2 XP damage

                   Medium LeetCode  5 XP damage
                   Hard LeetCode  7 XP damage
                   Extreme Challenge  10 XP damage
         Teams can choose whether to solve multiple easier problems or take the risk of attempting a
                  harder challenge for significantly more damage.
         The dragon can have a large health pool, such as 100 XP, and teams continuously attack it
                  until one team reduces its health to zero.
         The first team to defeat the dragon wins.
